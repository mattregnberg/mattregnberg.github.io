About

Matt is a film music composer, music producer, and visual director. Nominee of multiple international awards. With an early background in sound engineering, experience in recording studios and concert halls, and co-composing for various projects, he works across international film productions. He is involved in the creative process of commercials and film projects, contributing musically and through visual direction. His work has received strong feedback from high-profile industry professionals.
